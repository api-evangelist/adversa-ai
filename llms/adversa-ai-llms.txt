# Adversa AI

> Adversa AI is an autonomous AI red-teaming and security company (founded 2021, Tel Aviv) providing continuous security assessment for AI agents, LLMs, and GenAI applications. Its platform runs 300+ attack techniques (jailbreaks, prompt injection, adversarial attacks), is OWASP- and NIST-mapped, runs models on-prem, and prioritizes findings by business impact with auto-generated remediation.

This llms.txt was generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the catalog record. Adversa AI is a security-services company and does not publish a public developer API.
